Laraib, happy birthday. Before anything else, I want you to know that you have been a special person in my life.

From our first semester until now, we have shared so many things. Our vibes matched, we made videos, we spent time together, and there were moments when your care made me feel genuinely loved as a friend.

I remember the little ways you looked after me. I remember the kindness, the comfort, and the moments that made our friendship feel different. I do not want to pretend those things never happened, because they mattered to me.

But I also need to be honest. I have been hurt by moments when I felt that I was giving our friendship so much of myself, but was not receiving the same value in return. Sometimes I felt that other priorities came before me, and I did not know where I stood.

Maybe you never intended to hurt me. I am not claiming to know what was in your heart. I only know how some situations felt from my side, and how tired I became from holding those feelings.

I once wished that this friendship would prove to me that I was not unlucky in friendships. I wanted to feel secure, respected, and important too. When I did not feel that way, it affected me more deeply than I could explain.

I hope you have a beautiful birthday. I hope you receive kindness, peace, happiness, and all the good things you deserve. Thank you for the good moments we shared. I will always be able to appreciate what was real and meaningful.
